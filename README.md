# EMA News Reader

A SwiftUI app in development for reading news, events, and scientific guidelines from the European Medicines Agency (EMA).

The feed layer fetches and decodes all three feeds and converts their records into Swift models. The app opens to a placeholder screen and does not call the feed loaders yet.
